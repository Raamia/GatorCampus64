PLAY THE UF CAMPUS!

GatorCampus 64 is a native 3D co-op game built for SM64CoopDX. Explore Reitz and Marston, race the stadium, and play campus missions with friends. Turn a few photos into your playable gator, then bring it into the game.

1
Snap

1 to 4 photos of yourself: face, and an outfit shot if you like.

2
Gatorize

AI reads your look, Blender builds the model, and you watch it happen live.

3
Play

Your gator joins the guest roster on our server. Type /login in game to claim it.

MAKE YOUR PLAYER

PLAY ONLINE

Open sm64coopdx v1.5.1 with your own US Super Mario 64 ROM, install the GatorCampus 64 mod, then Join → Direct Connect:

play.gatorcamp.us:7777

In game, type /login and scan the QR code with your phone to link your account: your gator gets picked for you and your name tag gets a star. No QR? Use a link code.
